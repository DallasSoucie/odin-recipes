Candied Yams

Home

Description

These easy candied yams are a classic Southern Thanksgiving favorite made with canned yams, butter, brown sugar, and a golden marshmallow topping.

Ingredients

  • 1 (29 ounce) can yams or sweet potatoes, drained well
  • ¼ cup butter, cut into pieces
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 1 ½ cups miniature marshmallows

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F (200 degrees C).
  2. Arrange drained yams or sweet potatoes in an even layer in a medium baking dish. Dot evenly with butter.
  3. Sprinkle evenly with brown sugar, then cover with miniature marshmallows.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until sweet potatoes are heated through and marshmallows are melted and golden brown, about 20 to 25 minutes, watching closely to prevent burning.
  5. Enjoy!
